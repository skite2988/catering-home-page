Meet Chef Briana

Flavor with heart. Service fit for royalty.

Kitchen of Royalty was built from Chef Briana's passion for bringing people together through great food. For more than seven years, she has served everything from intimate gatherings to large corporate events with a focus on bold flavor, personal care, and dependable service.

From West Coast Mexican and barbecue to Southern Creole, soul food, breakfast, and brunch, the goal is simple: serve the stress-free feeling your guests and hosts remember.
